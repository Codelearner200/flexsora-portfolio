FAQ

Common questions.

How can AI automation help my business?

It handles repetitive tasks like data entry, routing and first-line replies, so your team can focus on higher-value work.

Will it work with my existing software?

Usually, if your tools offer an API or common integration. We confirm compatibility during discovery.

How long does a project take?

It depends on complexity. A simple workflow can be quick; multi-system projects take longer. We give an estimate after discovery.

How is my data handled?

We aim to minimise data access and discuss requirements up front. We do not claim certifications unless they are documented.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. Monitoring and maintenance can be included so workflows keep working as your tools change.

How does pricing work?

Pricing is based on scope. Request a quote and we will outline options.
